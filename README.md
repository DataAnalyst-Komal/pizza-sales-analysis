# Sales & Product Analysis Dashboard

## Overview

This project analyzes pizza sales data to identify revenue trends, product performance, and customer ordering patterns across different categories, sizes, and time periods.

## Objective

To analyze sales performance across pizza categories and sizes, identify top and bottom performing products, and uncover busiest sales periods to support data driven business decisions.

## Tech Stack

- **Power BI** — Dashboard design, data visualization & interactive reporting
- **Power Query** — Data cleaning & transformation
- **DAX** — Calculated columns & measures

## Dataset

- **Source:** Kaggle (publicly available Pizza Sales dataset)
- **Period:** January 2015 – December 2015
- **Fields include:** Order ID, order date, order time, pizza name, pizza category, pizza size, pizza ingredients, quantity and revenue

## Key Insights

- **Classic category drives maximum sales** at 26.91% — highest share across all categories

- **Large size pizzas lead demand** at 45.89% of total sales — bundle offers could increase avg order value

- **Friday is the busiest day** with 3.5K orders — staffing and stock levels should be optimized accordingly

- **July peaks in monthly orders** at 1,935 — seasonal promotions should be planned in advance

- **Thai Chicken Pizza drives highest revenue** at 43K — priority candidate for marketing promotions

- **Classic Deluxe Pizza leads in quantity and total orders** — strong customer preference indicates combo offer potential

- **Brie Carre Pizza is the lowest performer** across revenue, quantity and orders — candidate for repositioning or discontinuation

## Dashboard Features

- Sales trend analysis by day and month
- Pizza category and size performance breakdown
- Top 5 and Bottom 5 pizza analysis across revenue, quantity and total orders
- Interactive filters — Pizza Category and Date Range
- Insight panels with business recommendations
- Two page navigation — Home and Top/Bottom Sellers

## Business Impact

- Identifies best and worst performing products to guide menu decisions
- Supports targeted marketing for high revenue pizzas
- Enables smarter staffing and inventory planning based on peak periods
- Highlights underperforming products for repositioning or discontinuation
